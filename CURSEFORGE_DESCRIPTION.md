# Simple Bag Mover

Simple Bag Mover lets you reposition the default Blizzard bags in **WoW Forever** while keeping their familiar appearance.

Drag a bag by its title area to place it where you want. Each bag remembers its position when you close and reopen it, reload the UI, or log back in.

## Features

- Move individual Blizzard bags by dragging their title area.
- Save positions separately for each character.
- Restore saved positions after Blizzard rearranges the bag windows.
- Lock positions to prevent accidental dragging.
- Preserve the original item controls and right-click bag settings.
- Lightweight, with no dependencies.

## Commands

- `/sbm lock` — Lock bag positions.
- `/sbm unlock` — Enable dragging.
- `/sbm reset` — Restore the default Blizzard layout.
- `/sbm debug` — Show diagnostic information.

## Compatibility

Built exclusively for WoW Forever and its default Blizzard bags. Bank bags are currently outside the addon's scope.

Moving and position restoration pause during combat. Saved positions are reapplied after combat ends.
